01   OVERVIEWAI, Data & Automation

Generative AI

Generative AI creates new text, images and designs from patterns it has learned. For a business, the value comes from pointing it at your own data.

Hamdaz builds generative AI tools for organisations that want faster content, better customer conversations and fewer repetitive writing tasks. Our work covers AI-driven content creation, automated design and prototype generation, chatbot development and deepfake detection. We use OpenAI GPT models for natural language processing, and fine-tune them with your documents, product information and customer queries, so the output reflects your business rather than general internet text.

We start by gathering and reviewing the data the model will learn from, then train, test and connect it to the systems your teams already use, such as your website, CRM or internal portals. Security and compliance are set at the start, not added later, and models keep learning from new interactions after launch. Because Hamdaz also delivers cloud, data and cybersecurity services, one team can host the solution on Microsoft Azure or another cloud and support it over time. 01   OVERVIEWELV & Security Systems

CCTV & Video Surveillance

As an Abu Dhabi Monitoring & Control Centre (MCC) certified contractor, we design, install and hand over every CCTV system to MCC requirements. Each project starts with a site survey and risk assessment.

We then design camera coverage and field of view for the areas that matter: entrances and exits, lifts and lobbies, stairs and emergency exits, car parks and perimeters, cash and high-value areas, and loading bays and stores. You receive drawings and a BOQ, and we manage the design submission and NOC with MCC before installation begins.

We install fixed, PTZ, fisheye and multi-sensor IP cameras, indoor and outdoor rated, on PoE switching, fibre links and UPS. Recording runs on NVRs or servers sized to the required retention period. Video analytics cover line crossing, intrusion, people counting, face and ANPR. For live monitoring we build control rooms with VMS clients, operator desks and video walls. If you run an older analogue system, we migrate it to a compliant IP platform in phases. Commissioning includes recording verification, as-built drawings and MCC inspection sign-off.

01   OVERVIEWAudio Visual

Meeting Rooms & Video Conferencing

A video conferencing room should let anyone walk in, join a call and be seen and heard clearly. We design and build meeting rooms for organisations across the UAE, from focus and huddle rooms to executive boardrooms, divisible training rooms and town halls.

Each room runs on a certified platform: Microsoft Teams Rooms on Windows or Android, Zoom Rooms, Cisco Webex Room, Board and Desk series, or Google Meet hardware and ChromeOS room systems. Every room can also run BYOD and BYOM, so guests can share content or join a meeting from their own laptop.

We start with a site survey and needs analysis, then produce AV design drawings, a BOQ and room standards so every space of the same type works the same way. Our installers handle containment, cabling and mounting, and our control programmers build Crestron, Extron, AMX or Q-SYS interfaces in-house. DSP and UC programming, tuning and testing come before client sign-off. We then train your users and hand over as-built drawings and O&M manuals. Standardised rooms are easier for staff to use and simpler for IT to support, whether you have five rooms or a whole campus.
