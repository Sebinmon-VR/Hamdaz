LinkedIn section: options

Choose →

Three ways to show your LinkedIn posts on the home page, built from your 10 latest posts (their text, photos and dates). The cards are our own design, so they can be small and match the site. Every card opens the post on LinkedIn.

Point at the cards to see the hover effects. The marquee pauses while you point at it.

Option A · Marquee

Small cards moving slowly across the page

All 10 posts as compact cards (photo, date, title) in one row that drifts slowly, like the client logos higher up the home page. It pauses when you point at it.
